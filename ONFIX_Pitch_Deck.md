## SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM
**Heading:** Every Driver's Nightmare

**3 Pain Points:**

🚗 **Stranded on the Road**
Millions of drivers in Pakistan face vehicle breakdowns with no fast, reliable way to find help nearby.

🔍 **No Trusted Discovery**
Finding a mechanic is word-of-mouth or random Google searches — no ratings, no transparency, no accountability.

⏱️ **Wasted Time = Lost Money**
Average wait time for roadside help in Pakistan is 45–90 minutes. For mechanics, leads are inconsistent and unpredictable.

---

## SLIDE 3 — THE SOLUTION
**Heading:** ONFIX — The Mechanic Marketplace

**One Line:** ONFIX is a two-sided mobile platform that connects vehicle owners with verified nearby mechanics in real time.

**For Users:**
- Tap to find nearby mechanics (Bike, Car, Puncher)
- Real-time GPS tracking of mechanic location
- Request specific mechanics or broadcast to all nearby
- Book appointments in advance
- Rate and review after service

**For Mechanics:**
- Receive live service requests instantly
- Manage availability (Online/Offline toggle)
- Track earnings and service history
- Grow business with premium visibility plans

**Mechanic Side (3 Steps):**

## SLIDE 6 — PRODUCT — KEY FEATURES
**Heading:** Built for the Real World

**User App:**
- 🗺️ Nearby mechanics map with real-time distance & rating
- ⚡ Emergency request (broadcast to all nearby mechanics)
- 🎯 Selected mechanic request (pick a specific mechanic)
- 📅 Appointment booking system
- 💬 In-app service chat
- 📍 Live GPS tracking of mechanic en route
- 🔔 Real-time push notifications (Firebase FCM)
- 📜 Request history & cancellation management

**Mechanic App:**
- 📊 Earnings dashboard (daily, monthly)
- 🔴 Online/Offline presence toggle
- 📦 Subscription plan management
- 🏆 Performance-based rating & badge system
- 🔔 Instant request alerts with sound & vibration
- 🗺️ Live navigation to user location

**Technology Stack:**
- Flutter (iOS + Android)
- Spring Boot backend (Google Cloud Run)
- WebSocket (STOMP) for real-time communication
- Google Maps API
- Firebase (FCM push notifications)
- Redis (request tracking)
- Integrated payment gateway (Swich)
